Privacy Policy

This application accesses only the account holder's own bank account data, through Enable Banking's PSD2 account-information API, and only with the account holder's explicit consent.

Retrieved data is stored on a private server controlled solely by the account holder. It is not shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party. The application is read-only and cannot initiate payments.

The account holder may revoke access at any time via their bank or via Enable Banking's consent management.
